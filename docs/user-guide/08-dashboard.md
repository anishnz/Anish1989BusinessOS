# 8. Dashboard

*User guide, chapter 8 · Last updated 22 October 2026*

**Who this is for:** business owners and managers.

Click **Dashboard** at the top right of your business admin (or go to `/dashboard/`). It shows how today is going and what needs attention.

You need the **Reports – View** permission. The owner and manager roles have it by default (for example Business Owner, System Admin, Operations Manager, Sales Manager, Finance Manager, Inventory Manager, Department Manager), and so do Accountant and Auditor. Shop Staff do not.

## 8.1 What the numbers mean

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sales today** | Totals of orders **completed** (picked up) today, NZ$ including GST. The line below compares with the **same day last week** and shows the GST included. |
| **Orders confirmed** | Orders confirmed today that were not cancelled or expired. |
| **Average sale** | Sales today divided by the number of completed orders. |
| **Completed, not paid yet** | Completed orders today still marked as not paid. Mark payments on each order (chapter 6.5). |
| **Unread WhatsApp chats** | Open conversations with new messages (only shown if you can see customers). |

A green ▲ means higher than the same day last week, and a red ▼ means lower.

A day runs from midnight to midnight, New Zealand time.

## 8.2 Sales, last 7 days

One bar per day, today on the right with its value. Point at a bar (or use the Tab key) to see its exact amount. Click **Show as a table** to see all seven numbers.

## 8.3 Top products, last 7 days

The five products with the most sales in completed orders, with the amount sold (kg, packs or items) and how many orders included them.

## 8.4 Orders needing attention

How many orders are waiting at each step: awaiting the customer's confirmation, confirmed (to prepare), being prepared, final price sent or accepted, ready, and out for delivery. Click a step to open those orders. **Pickups due** and **Deliveries due** count today's booked orders (not drafts) with a time today that are not completed yet.

## 8.5 Another day

Choose a date in **Day** and click **Show**. **Back to today** returns to today.

More reports and charts (customer analysis, trends, exports) come with the business intelligence module.
