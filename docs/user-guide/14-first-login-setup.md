# 14. First-login setup

*User guide, chapter 14 · Last updated 21 October 2026*

**Who this is for:** the System Admin and IT Admin of a new business (people with **Settings – Configure**).

The first time you open the admin pages of a new business, a short setup opens: **Set up your business**. It has four steps. You can skip any step and come back later.

## 14.1 The steps

| Step | What you do | Where to change it later |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Business details | Trading name, phone, email for customers, address, GST number, website. All optional. | **Set up your business** at the top of the admin pages |
| 2. Backups | Set the backup passphrase, connect your Google Drive, make a test backup | **Backups** (chapter 12) |
| 3. Connections | See which accounts are connected: email sending, WhatsApp, an AI model (optional). Click **Add** to connect one. | **Integrations** (chapter 4) |
| 4. Done | A summary of what is done and what is left. Click **Finish setup**. | – |

Checks on business details:
- Phone: numbers only, for example `09 123 4567` or `+64 21 123 4567`.
- GST number: 8 or 9 digits, for example `123-456-789`.
- Postcode: 4 digits.
- Website: `www.example.co.nz` is fine. `https://` is added for you.

## 14.2 The backups step

1. **Backup passphrase.** Enter your password and click **Make a passphrase for me**. Save the passphrase now (it is shown once), then click **I have saved it**. You come back to the setup.
2. **Google Drive.** Enter your password and click **Connect Google Drive**. Sign in to Google and allow access. You come back to the setup.
3. **Test backup.** Click **Make a test backup**. The page updates by itself. When it shows **✓ Backup working**, the backup was made, encrypted and copied to your Google Drive.

If **Connect Google Drive** is not offered, the platform's Google sign-in app is not set up yet. Skip the step and ask the platform Super Admin.

## 14.3 Later

- **Later – go to the admin home page** closes the setup until you sign in again.
- After **Finish setup**, it does not open by itself any more. Open it again with **Set up your business** at the top of the admin pages.
- Until backups are set up, a yellow reminder is shown at the top of every admin page: **Backups to your Google Drive are not set up yet.** People who cannot change settings see "Ask your IT Admin or System Admin to set them up."

Only System Admins and IT Admins see the setup. Other staff go straight to their pages.
