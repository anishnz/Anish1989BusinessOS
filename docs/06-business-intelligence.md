# 06. Business intelligence

Last updated: 21 October 2026

## Goal

Give owners many clear charts and a full picture of how customers respond, so they can make better decisions. Every number shows its source, date range and calculation.

## Chart types

| Chart | Example use |
|---|---|
| KPI cards | Today's sales, gross profit, orders, average order value |
| Line chart | Sales over time, this week vs last week |
| Bar chart | Sales by product or category |
| Stacked bar | Sales by channel (WhatsApp, website, in-store) |
| Pie or donut | Share of sales by category |
| Funnel | QR scans → chats → confirmed orders → repeat orders |
| Heatmap | Busy hours by day of the week |
| Cohort chart | How many new customers come back each month |
| Gauge | Progress to monthly sales target |
| Map | Customers or competitors by suburb |
| Scatter | Price vs margin per product |
| Table and pivot | Detailed numbers with filters and export |

## Dashboard features

- Drag-and-drop widgets, saved per user or role, with lockable layouts
- Date range filters and comparison to a previous period
- Drill-down from a chart to the underlying records
- Export to CSV and PDF
- Scheduled reports by email or WhatsApp
- Natural language questions to the AI analyst ("Which products had the best margin last week?"), with the calculation explained

## Customer response analytics

| Source | Metrics |
|---|---|
| Surveys | NPS (net promoter score), CSAT (customer satisfaction) |
| Reviews and ratings | Average rating, rating trend, review topics |
| Chat and message replies | Reply time, resolution time, sentiment, common questions, handover rate |
| Campaigns and coupons | Sent, opened, clicked, redeemed, sales from campaign, opt-outs |
| Post-order feedback | Star rating after each order and comment topics |

## Advanced analytics

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer segments (RFM) | Loyal, new, at risk and lost, based on how recently, how often and how much each customer buys |
| Basket analysis | Products often bought together, for bundles and offers |
| Demand forecast | Expected sales by product next week, to reduce wastage. Shows its assumptions and confidence. |
| Anomaly alerts | Alerts when a number is unusual, for example "Sales are 30% lower than a normal Tuesday" |
| Daily owner summary | Evening WhatsApp message: sales, top products, low stock, open orders |
| Competitor comparison | Uses data from [Competitor intelligence](07-competitor-intelligence.md) |

## Rules

- The BI module only shows data the user is allowed to see.
- Referral income (platform revenue) stays separate from client sales and profit.
- Forecasts and AI explanations are labelled as estimates.
