# Business Brainstorm: Anish1989 — AI-Powered Business Growth Ecosystem

Your idea has the potential to become more than a website development service. You could build a performance-based digital transformation business for small and medium-sized businesses in Auckland, starting with restaurants, butcheries, cafés, grocery stores, and other local businesses.

The core concept is simple:

You help traditional businesses become intelligent, automated, data-driven businesses using websites, business intelligence, AI agents, and digital marketing. You reduce their initial technology costs, help them grow, and earn a commission when the agreed business results are achieved.

Your proposed website, `www.anish1989.com.np`, becomes your personal brand, business portfolio, demonstration platform, and lead-generation engine.

### How to prevent discount abuse

Build the system with unique, expiring coupon codes; server-side eligibility checks; transaction limits; redemption logs; and optional POS integration. Avoid relying on a screenshot of a coupon as proof of redemption.

The bigger opportunity: sell this as a reusable customer engagement platform. One QR engine can serve hundreds of participating businesses, each with its own branding, campaigns, and reporting.

## 6. Your 3% performance-based business model

Your proposed offer is attractive because it reduces the client's initial financial risk. However, the definition of profit needs to be precise.

I recommend starting with a pilot agreement that combines an initial demonstration with a measurable performance fee.

### How you could earn revenue

| Revenue stream        | How it works                                                           |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Performance fee       | A negotiated share of verified incremental profit                      |
| Monthly management    | Optional fee for hosting, maintenance, and operational support         |
| AI subscriptions      | Client pays the relevant AI, automation, or software provider directly |
| Marketing services    | Optional video production, campaigns, and content management           |
| Platform subscription | Future recurring fee for your reusable management and QR system        |
| Integration and setup | Optional fee for complex POS, accounting, or other integrations        |

You can initially waive setup fees for selected pilot clients, but do not commit to unlimited free development and support.

### Why defining the 3% matters

Suppose a participating business agrees that you receive 3% of the verified incremental profit attributable to your system.

A $60 monthly fee may not cover substantial software development, ongoing support, AI subscriptions, or marketing. Therefore, you should consider a minimum monthly fee, a defined pilot period, or a hybrid model once you have proven the results.

Before offering this commercially, have a New Zealand accountant or lawyer help define:

- The baseline period and how incremental profit is calculated.
- Whether refunds, discounts, additional stock, labour, advertising, and software costs are deducted.
- How external factors, seasonality, and other marketing activities are handled.
- What records the business must provide to verify the calculation.
- Payment timing, audit rights, termination, data ownership, and responsibility for ongoing maintenance.

Do not guarantee a profit increase that you cannot control. Your agreement should distinguish between the improvements you deliver and the business outcomes that depend on the owner's products, pricing, staffing, and other decisions.

## 9. Technology stack: build fast without creating technical debt

Since your concept depends on vibe coding, choose tools that let you build demonstrations quickly but retain control of the underlying data and business logic.

| Layer                       | Suggested approach                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal website and demos  | Base44, Claude-assisted development, or another suitable AI coding platform         |
| Custom application          | React/Next.js or a suitable full-stack framework                                    |
| Database and authentication | PostgreSQL with managed authentication and row-level access controls                |
| AI agents                   | Model APIs with tool permissions, workflow orchestration, and audit logs            |
| Business automation         | n8n or equivalent workflow automation                                               |
| Business intelligence       | SQL-based metrics and dashboards; add a dedicated BI tool when needed               |
| QR campaigns                | A reusable web application with server-validated coupon codes                       |
| Hosting and security        | Managed cloud hosting, backups, monitoring, secrets management, and access controls |

You do not need every component on day one. For your initial demonstration, one AI coding platform and a simple database-backed application may be sufficient.

For a production system, check that your chosen platform supports data export, secure authentication, custom integrations, reliable backups, and appropriate control over application code. The client should own their business data and retain access to their domains and critical accounts.

## 11. The most important business risks to address

Before taking the first client live, account for these issues:

- Profit attribution: Establish an agreed baseline and a transparent way to measure incremental profit.
- Privacy: Follow New Zealand privacy requirements, particularly when collecting customer details or using AI to process personal information.
- Marketing consent: Respect unsubscribe requests and the rules governing commercial electronic messages.
- Platform restrictions: Social media, messaging, and AI services have different API access, usage limits, and subscription costs. Not every integration will be available on every plan.
- Security: Separate each client's data, use least-privilege access, and maintain logs, backups, and recovery procedures.
- Business continuity: Define who owns the website, code, domain, AI accounts, customer data, and ongoing maintenance.
- Your capacity: Free setup can become expensive if you build a separate custom application for every business. Reuse templates and limit the scope of each pilot.

Because this is a performance-based commercial arrangement, have the contract and accounting model reviewed by appropriate New Zealand professionals before signing clients.
