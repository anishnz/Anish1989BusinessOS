## 1. How the referral system works

Step 1 — Share

You or a participating business shares a referral link through a website, QR code, social media post, or personal invitation.

Step 2 — Track

The system records referral clicks and campaign identifiers, subject to appropriate privacy and tracking consent.

Step 3 — Verify

The system checks whether a referral resulted in an eligible registration, purchase, or other qualifying action. Confirmed conversions should come from the provider where possible.

Step 4 — Record earnings

Record the expected commission, confirmed commission, payment status, and any reversal or cancellation. Do not count a click or unconfirmed sign-up as earned income.

## 3. Referral dashboard

The administrator should be able to create and manage referral campaigns without changing application code.

| Feature             | Required functionality                                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Referral providers  | Wise and other approved services                        |
| Campaign management | Name, description, destination URL, start/end dates     |
| Referral codes      | Unique codes for businesses, partners, or campaigns     |
| Click tracking      | Click count, source, campaign, and timestamp            |
| Conversion tracking | Pending, confirmed, rejected, reversed                  |
| Commission ledger   | Expected, approved, paid, and outstanding               |
| QR code generator   | Generate a QR code for each referral campaign           |
| Reporting           | Click-through rate, conversion rate, confirmed earnings |
| Compliance          | Terms, disclosures, consent, and campaign restrictions  |

For providers without a conversion API, allow manual reconciliation using the provider's referral dashboard. Never claim that your system can independently verify a conversion if the provider does not share that information.

## 4. How it connects to your other business modules

- QR games: Customers can scan a store QR code to access a discount or a disclosed referral offer.
- Business owners: Participating businesses can share approved referral campaigns with customers.
- AI marketing agent: Draft referral posts and promotional messages for your approval.
- Business intelligence: Track referral performance and confirmed earnings separately from client sales and profit.
- Community portal: In a later phase, eligible partners or students could participate in a transparent referral programme, subject to provider rules.

### Recommended implementation order

1. MVP: Admin-managed referral links, QR codes, click tracking, and manual commission records.
2. Phase 2: Individual referral codes, partner accounts, conversion imports, and automated reports.
3. Phase 3: Provider integrations, configurable reward rules, fraud detection, and multi-business referral campaigns.

One important accounting distinction: referral income belongs in your platform's own revenue reporting, not in a client's incremental-profit calculation. Keep your earnings, client sales, and customer rewards as separate financial records.
